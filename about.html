I’m a public policy analyst with experience in public finance, energy, and development policy. I’ve led impactful research and advocacy projects in Tunisia and the United States, and I hold a Master’s in Public Policy from the Willy Brandt School in Germany.

My work focuses on advancing the energy transition in resource-constrained settings, especially where water scarcity, debt, and political fragmentation pose major challenges. I’m currently working on projects at the intersection of water and energy policy.

This site is a space to share what I’ve built, explored, and continue to learn in the pursuit of sustainable futures.
