The Solar Revolution: Policy Frameworks Driving Adoption

Published June 15, 2023 • 8 min read

The global solar energy landscape has transformed dramatically over the past decade. Thanks to falling costs and increasing policy support, solar photovoltaics (PV) have become a cornerstone of the energy transition in many countries.

This post explores the types of policies that have enabled this growth — including feed-in tariffs, tax credits, auction mechanisms, and net metering — and evaluates their relative effectiveness in different political and economic contexts.

As emerging markets ramp up their solar ambitions, understanding what works — and what doesn't — can help design smarter, more equitable clean energy strategies.
