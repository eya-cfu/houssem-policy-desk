Tunisia in the Mediterranean Green Hydrogen Race

Published April 4, 2025 • 8 min read

Europe’s green hydrogen push has created new geopolitical opportunities — and pressures — for its neighbors. Tunisia, with its high solar potential and proximity to European markets, is emerging as a key player in the region’s hydrogen strategies.

But can Tunisia seize this opportunity without replicating extractive energy dynamics of the past? This post examines the potential and the political economy constraints, from land rights and desalination needs to interconnection infrastructure and labor impacts.

We also look at how Tunisia compares with other frontrunners like Morocco, Egypt, and Chile — and what kind of policy architecture would make green hydrogen work *for Tunisians*, not just European supply chains.
