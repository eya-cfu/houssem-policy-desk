Water Scarcity and Decarbonization in the Global South

Published April 4, 2025 • 7 min read

Water is the hidden backbone of the energy transition — yet in many countries in the Global South, water is running out. Green hydrogen, solar cooling systems, and renewable desalination all depend on a reliable water supply.

This post outlines why decarbonization policies must integrate water resilience from the start. In Tunisia, where per capita water availability is among the lowest in the world, the link between water policy and energy transition is no longer optional — it's existential.

We look at the latest innovations in solar-powered desalination and how financing mechanisms could prioritize dual-impact infrastructure that solves for both energy and water simultaneously.
