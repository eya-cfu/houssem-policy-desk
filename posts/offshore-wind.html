Water, Energy, and Scarcity: A Policy Nexus

Published: April 4, 2025

Water scarcity is no longer a distant threat—it’s a daily reality for millions. But its connection to the energy transition remains underexplored in policymaking, especially in water-stressed regions like North Africa and the Middle East.

This post outlines how desalination, hydropower, and solar-powered water systems are reshaping the water-energy policy landscape. We also examine the risks of over-reliance on energy-intensive water solutions.

Recommendations include investing in regional water-sharing mechanisms, leveraging green finance, and integrating water metrics into national energy transition plans.
