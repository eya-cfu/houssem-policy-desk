Offshore Wind: Navigating the Regulatory Seas

Published May 28, 2023 • 12 min read

Offshore wind is rapidly emerging as a major player in the global clean energy mix. But its deployment comes with unique regulatory challenges — from permitting and environmental approvals to grid interconnection and market design.

This article examines the regulatory approaches of Europe, Asia, and North America, identifying key patterns and outliers in how countries navigate the political, technical, and economic dimensions of offshore wind.

The post concludes with a set of policy recommendations for countries seeking to streamline their offshore wind strategies while maintaining environmental safeguards and public trust.
